Services offered

Counselling, shaped around what you need.

Individuals

Personal growth counselling

For anyone working through a difficult stretch, a pattern they want to change, or simply wanting to understand themselves more clearly. This is space to slow down and think out loud with someone trained to listen closely and ask the right questions.

Couples

Couples counselling

For partners who want to understand each other again — whether you're working through a specific conflict, a rough patch, or just want a steadier way of communicating going forward.

Families

Family counselling

For families navigating conflict, big transitions, or simply wanting to strengthen how they communicate with one another. Sessions can include as many family members as makes sense for the situation.

How sessions work

1 hr
Every session is a full hour, held remotely by video. All you need is a private space and an internet connection. Sessions are booked directly with me — reach out and we'll find a time that works.
