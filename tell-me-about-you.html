Tell me about you

Let's find the right support for you

Everyone comes to guidance for a different reason. These questions will help us understand where you are, what you're navigating, and what kind of support may be most helpful.

  1. What brings you here today?
    • Personal growth
    • Starting over or a major life transition
    • Structure, balance, or goal setting
    • Couples guidance
    • Family relationships
    • Women's empowerment
    • I'm not sure yet
  2. What would you most like help with right now?
    • Gaining clarity or direction
    • Feeling more confident
    • Creating structure and better routines
    • Setting and reaching goals
    • Improving communication
    • Understanding a relationship
    • Working through conflict
    • Adjusting to a life change
    • Reconnecting with myself
    • Something else
  3. How would you describe where you are right now?
    • I feel stuck and don't know what comes next.
    • I know what I want but need help getting there.
    • I'm going through a major change.
    • Things are generally okay, but I want to grow.
    • I'm struggling within a relationship or family situation.
    • I mainly need someone to listen and help me sort things out.
  4. If things were going better three months from now, what would be different?
  5. What do you feel is your biggest obstacle right now?
  6. What would you most like to walk away from our time together with?

Not sure which service fits? That's completely okay — your answers will simply help us determine the type of guidance that best fits where you are and what you'd like to work toward.
