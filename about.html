About me

Hi, I'm Mary.

I work with individuals, couples, and families who are ready to look honestly at what's going on in their lives and do something about it. My style is warm and direct — I'll listen closely, and I'll also tell you what I'm noticing, because I think that's part of what makes this work useful.

I believe most people already have more insight into their own situation than they give themselves credit for. My job is less about handing over answers and more about helping you find the ones that were already taking shape — whether that's in a family working through conflict, a couple trying to understand each other again, or someone on their own doing the quieter work of personal growth.

All of my sessions are remote, held over video, and run a full hour. That's been a deliberate choice: it lowers the barrier to actually showing up, and it means we're not rushing.

I hold a Bachelor of Social Work, which shapes how I listen and how I think about the people I work with. I'm not a licensed therapist or counselor, and this isn't a substitute for licensed mental health treatment — it's guidance and support for people who want a steady space to think things through.
